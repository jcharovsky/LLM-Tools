#### Universities

We are trying to make DBeaver convenient for all user groups including those who are only starting to get acquainted with database management. With DBeaver, the learning process becomes easier and more enjoyable.

We are happy that we can facilitate academic activities in universities and support scientific communities and students. That is why we grant free DBeaver EE licenses to students and are ready to collaborate with universities.

Are you a student? [Join](/academic-license/) the DBeaver club!
