#### Academic License

Are you studying relational or NoSQL databases or just planning to do so?  
We are ready to help you with that by giving you a free academic license for the Enterprise version of DBeaver or CloudBeaver!  
You just need to fill in the request form below.

Please note that the most important information for us to make a positive decision regarding your request is your real university email.  
As soon as we validate your data, we will send you an email with the free academic license in it. Usually, it takes us not more than three working days.

You need to login or register a new account

You should

[Sign in](https://dbeaver.com/signin/?redirect_to=%2Facademic-license%2F)

or

[Sign up](https://dbeaver.com/signup/?redirect_to=%2Facademic-license%2F)

on our site.
